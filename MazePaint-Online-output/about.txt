🎮 About MazePaint

Welcome to MazePaint!

MazePaint is a challenging puzzle game in the maze genre, where you must find the correct path and make your way through the labyrinth.

✨ Features:
• A variety of levels with different difficulty settings
• Star and scoring system
• Record tracking and best-time records
• Player leaderboard
• Compete with other players
• Levels ranging from easy to extremely difficult
• Simple, smooth design for fast performance

📌 Before Installation:
To ensure the game runs properly, make sure you have enough free space on your system and that the installation file has been downloaded completely.

🚀 After installation, launch the game, choose your player name, and begin your MazePaint adventure!

Good luck, and beat your own record! 🏆
